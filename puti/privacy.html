Puti Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Puti is designed to help you remember where you put your everyday items. Your privacy is important to us.

Information You Add to Puti

Puti allows you to save information about your belongings, including item names, locations, notes, and optional photos.

How Your Data Is Stored

Information you add to Puti is stored locally on your device. Puti does not require you to create an account, and your saved item information is not transmitted to Eze Labs or stored on Eze Labs servers.

Photos

Puti may request access to your photo library when you choose to add a photo to an item. Photo access is used only to allow you to select and associate a photo with an item you save in the app.

Data Collection

Puti does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties.

Advertising and Tracking

Puti does not contain third-party advertising and does not use your information for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Children's Privacy

Puti does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because Puti stores its app data locally on the user's device, Eze Labs does not receive personal information entered into the app.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Puti evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
